About VioletTech

Built by a specialist.
Backed by Microsoft.

VioletTech Solutions is a registered technology consultancy founded by Lyborn Nemo, a Microsoft Power Platform specialist based in Johannesburg, South Africa, holding the complete Power Platform certification stack — PL-900 through PL-600.

With a BSc in Computer Science and hands-on experience building production systems across Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Power Pages, Copilot Studio, and Dynamics 365, VioletTech delivers the technical depth of an enterprise consultancy with the agility of a specialist partner.

We serve clients across South Africa, Australia, the United Kingdom, and the United States — remotely and on-site — bringing Microsoft-certified expertise to organisations of all sizes.
